VIDEO POKER — GAMES, PAY TABLES & VARIATIONS
=============================================
All payouts are PER COIN bet. The Royal Flush (or Natural Royal) pays a
jackpot bonus at max coins: 250/coin at 1-4 coins, 800/coin (4000 total)
at 5 coins, except where noted. Returns assume optimal play at max coins.

Returns shown are EXACT expected values, not simulations: computed by
strategy/strategy-gen.js over every one of the 2,598,960 possible deals
(2,869,685 for the 53-card joker games) and cross-checked against the
published Wizard of Odds figures. All 54 tables below have a generated
strategy guide in the app (Settings -> Strategy Guide).

16 games, 54 pay tables. A side-by-side payout comparison of every game's
top variant is in video-poker-payouts.xlsx.
Last refreshed: 2026-09-06

=========================== NOTES & CORRECTIONS ==========================

Two data corrections were made on 2026-09-06 after every table's return was
computed exactly and compared with Wizard of Odds:

  * SUPER DOUBLE DOUBLE BONUS — bare face quads were paying 80; the real
    game pays 50 unless there is an A/J/Q/K kicker (which pays 160). The
    8/5 variant had been reading 100.87%, which no real machine offers.

  * DEUCES WILD "Airport" — the straight flush was listed at 10; the
    98.91% Airport/Illinois table pays 9. Now 25/15/9/4/4/3.

The two joker Kings-or-Better figures round up rather than down from the
values often quoted (100.6463% -> 100.65%, 98.9372% -> 98.94%); the
underlying numbers agree with Wizard of Odds exactly.
